Slide 2 · Opportunity1:15

What happens after the property purchase completes?

The commercial opportunity starts after completion. Owners still need inspections, records, repairs and coordination, but that work is usually fragmented across messages, documents and different providers. Our proposed split is simple. Gingervale owns the client, sets the Owner Care offer and remains the trusted human contact. Kivilo provides the shared digital record, evidence, approvals and signatures. A UK operating partner performs any work that needs somebody on site. Gingervale is not reselling anonymous software; it is offering a managed service supported by technology.

Three features available today

This is the working core today, not a concept mock-up. First, the inspector captures each room with photos and a walkthrough. Second, they mark the exact detail that matters while the original photograph remains untouched. Third, the room inventory and evidence become a dated report that both parties sign and later compare at check-out. The product is still in private beta and iOS approval is pending, but this complete condition-report workflow is working now.

Slide 6 · Repairs1:00

From report to repair resolution

The signed report is the starting point for ongoing Owner Care. When something breaks, the tenant logs the problem and adds evidence. The parties record who will arrange the work and who is responsible for the cost. After the repair, the tenant confirms whether it is fixed, and the full sequence remains on the property timeline. This workflow is already built. It turns a one-off inspection into a continuing service Gingervale can manage for the owner.

Slide 7 · Roadmap1:20

Five modules. We have started at M4.

What you have just seen is M4: Condition and Care, currently in working private beta. The repair workflow is the first working slice of M5. M1 to M3—deal creation, signing chains, and AML and CDD—are planned, not being presented as finished. The strategic value is that these are not five disconnected tools. They reuse the same property, owner, tenant and agent record. Every completed workflow makes the next one easier to start. Today is the wedge; the larger opportunity is the shared operating layer around the full property relationship.

Do not promise dates. Be precise about working, begun and planned.
Slide 8 · Partner terms1:30

Start free. Pay as the portfolio grows.

The commercial structure protects the upside for Gingervale. The eight-week founding pilot has no software fee and covers up to 10 active properties. If the model works, the proposed founding rate is S$39 per user each month, including the first 25 active properties. Properties 26 to 100 cost S$2 each per month, with a lower volume tier to be agreed above that. Gingervale sets and keeps the Owner Care fee; Kivilo does not take a revenue percentage. The planned public Kivilo Pro price is S$49 per user, so this gives Gingervale a permanent founding discount and more margin per managed property.

Land: Gingervale owns the client revenue. Kivilo earns predictable software revenue.

Slide 10 · Close1:25

A small test with a clear decision at the end

Our proposal is to test this with up to 10 property records over eight weeks. There is no software fee, no exclusivity and no revenue share. Gingervale covers its own client operations and fieldwork; we provide the software, setup support and product iteration. The first working session should select one owner journey, assign responsibilities and agree two or three success measures—for example owner uptake, time required per property and whether the service creates a credible recurring offer. At the end of eight weeks, we make a clear continue-or-stop decision.
