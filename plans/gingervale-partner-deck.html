TIME 1:15. The commercial opportunity starts after completion. Owners still need inspections, records, repairs and coordination, but that work is usually fragmented across messages, documents and different providers. Our proposed split is simple. Gingervale owns the client, sets the Owner Care offer and remains the trusted human contact. Kivilo provides the shared digital record, evidence, approvals and signatures. A UK operating partner performs any work that needs somebody on site. Gingervale is not reselling anonymous software; it is offering a managed service supported by technology.

TIME 1:30. The commercial structure protects the upside for Gingervale. The eight-week founding pilot has no software fee and covers up to 10 active properties. If the model works, the proposed founding rate is S$39 per user each month, including the first 25 active properties. Properties 26 to 100 cost S$2 each per month, with a lower volume tier to be agreed above that. Gingervale sets and keeps the Owner Care fee; Kivilo does not take a revenue percentage. The planned public Kivilo Pro price is S$49 per user, so this gives Gingervale a permanent founding discount and more margin per managed property.

TIME 1:25. Our proposal is to test this with up to 10 property records over eight weeks. There is no software fee, no exclusivity and no revenue share. Gingervale covers its own client operations and fieldwork; we provide the software, setup support and product iteration. The first working session should select one owner journey, assign responsibilities and agree two or three success measures—for example owner uptake, time required per property and whether the service creates a credible recurring offer. At the end of eight weeks, we make a clear continue-or-stop decision. Close with: Can we agree to scope that pilot together as the next step?
