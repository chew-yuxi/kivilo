kivilo

Close more. Chase less. See everything.

Kivilo automates the admin behind every real estate transaction and gives every stakeholder a live watchtower over the properties they care about. Less friction per deal. More deals per agent.

Singapore first. Built for the agents who close, the landlords who own, and the tenants who live there.

01The problem

Every deal drags a tail of manual admin. Every property sits in a blind spot.

An agent's real job is closing. Instead they spend evenings re-typing the same facts into five documents, herding signatures over WhatsApp, and photographing handovers nobody can find two years later. And once the deal closes, nobody can see the property anymore.

Friction at closing

5+ documents per transaction, assembled by hand from chat messages and old PDFs. AML/CDD is mandatory under the 2021 regulations, and mostly done as checkbox theatre.

Friction at handover

Move-in condition lives in a camera roll. Two years later the deposit dispute is two people squinting at photos nobody indexed.

No watchtower

Co-owners, tenant, agent, property manager, the tenant's employer: all need oversight of the same unit. Each keeps a partial copy. Every handoff loses state.

02The solution

Five modules. Each one makes the next free to start.

One property record underneath. Enter the facts once, and documents, signatures, compliance checks and inspections all run off it.

08The rollout

Check-in and check-out is what you can use first.

First

AI check-in / check-out

The one module that needs nothing else to be useful: film the walkthrough on your next handover, get the signed condition report back. No migration, no setup, and every report you file quietly starts a property record.

Next

Deal wizard and signing chains

The closing pack generated from those records, then routed for signature in the right order. Zero re-typed fields between documents.

Then

Compliance and the watchtower

Screening and pass verification run themselves on every new stakeholder, and every landlord, tenant and manager you invite gets their own view of the property.

We are onboarding the first agents now. Your next handover can be the pilot.
