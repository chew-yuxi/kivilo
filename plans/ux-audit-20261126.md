# Kivilo mobile handover UX audit

26 November 2026 · Agent workflow with client checks paused

A new agent can go from Home to **Start check-in** without encountering identity verification. A returning agent can resume at a named property. Inside a check-in, “1/2 rooms reviewed” sits next to the name of the room to open next. At signing, each party has their own status and email history. Keep these cues when changing the creation flow. [Nine mobile screen captures](ux-audit-20261126.png)

## Findings in order of priority

| Priority | What the agent experiences | Recommended change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | **Leaving the deposit blank records a zero deposit.** I submitted a check-in for an existing property with rent and deposit blank. Both optional fields reached the API as `0`. Publishing a check-out uses that stored deposit to calculate the proposed refund. | Preserve an omitted amount as unknown. Let the inspection proceed, then ask the agent for the deposit or an explicit zero before publishing a deposit statement. Existing zeros need confirmation before treating them as known. |
| High | **Create can fail with the explanation offscreen.** I filled the required names and address, left furnishing unselected and tapped the fixed Create button. The form stayed open. “Choose the property furnishing level” became visible only after scrolling further down. | Bring the first missing field into view, put its message beside the control and announce it to screen readers. Verify this from both ends of the form with the keyboard open. |
| Medium | **The main button labels are hard to read.** White on the default pink (`#EC4899`) measures 3.53:1. WCAG AA calls for 4.5:1 for ordinary text. | Use the existing dark pink (`#BE185D`, 6.04:1 with white) for filled buttons. Check pressed and focused states separately. [WCAG contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) |
| Low | **Home says “Needs your action · 3” above two rows.** In the returning-agent example, the third task is promoted to “Continue where you left off” above the heading. | Count the rows under that heading. If space limits the list, show the total on “View all”. |

For a second market, address and money are the next visible barriers. The new-property form requires a postal code, and the optional rent and deposit fields say SGD. Tie address validation and currency to the property's country before offering the flow there.

## How this was checked

I rendered nine views of the Flutter app at 390 × 844 logical pixels with synthetic properties and parties, and exercised the two form failures in the widget harness. The captures show different fixture properties at different stages. Network responses and the system font were supplied by the harness. Physical-device capture, live email, offline recovery and sessions with working agents were outside this review.

The form submission and error placement are in [New check-in](../mobile/lib/screens/new_inspection_screen.dart). The saved deposit feeds [assessment publication](../src/lib/assessment-actions.ts) and the [deposit calculation](../src/lib/assessment-schema.ts). Button colors come from the [mobile theme](../mobile/lib/ui/kivilo_theme.dart).

## 27 November follow-up

The check-in keeps blank rent and deposit fields blank. If the deposit is unknown, the agent enters the actual amount or an explicit zero when publishing the deposit statement. That amount belongs to the dated statement. The original signed inventory retains its fingerprint. A zero saved by an older client requires confirmation.

Create now focuses the first invalid field and scrolls it into view. The furnishing message appears above its choices. Home counts the rows under each heading, and filled buttons use the existing dark pink. The matched phone captures show the same tap, viewport and tenancy before and after the changes. [See the four screens](ux-handover-20261127.png)

In the local Malaysian pilot, both parties signed the check-in and the check-out with the deposit blank. The agent then entered RM2,000 before publishing a RM250 finding. The tenant’s statement showed RM1,750 to return, and its two check-out signatures still matched.
